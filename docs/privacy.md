# Oscuru privacy policy

Oscuru is a camera and photo editor for iPhone. It has no server and no account of its own, and it collects nothing about you.

## Your photos

Your photos, their edits and your conversations with the assistant are kept on your iPhone, in Oscuru's own storage. They leave it only in the two ways below.

## The assistant

The assistant is a language model that you reach through your own OpenRouter account. You connect that account in Oscuru's Settings, and the key it gives Oscuru is kept in your iPhone's keychain.

When you ask the assistant for something, Oscuru sends it, through OpenRouter, to the model you chose:

- your words,
- smaller copies of the photo (and of anything you attach),
- the photo's settings and what it says about itself, such as camera, lens and exposure.

OpenRouter and the model's provider handle this under their own privacy policies and under the settings of your OpenRouter account. Oscuru sends nothing to them when you are not using the assistant.

## Saving and sharing

When you save a photo to your photo library or share it, it goes where you sent it. Oscuru asks for permission to add photos to your library and does not read the library.

## The camera

Oscuru uses the camera only to take the photos you take.

## Crash reports and feedback

If you test Oscuru through TestFlight, Apple may send its developer crash reports and the feedback you choose to send, as TestFlight describes. Oscuru itself sends nothing.

## No tracking

Oscuru has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Questions

Ask through TestFlight's feedback, or the feedback email shown in TestFlight.
